# ⚡ Market News AI
### AI-Powered Stock Market Catalyst & Impact Intelligence Platform

> **From breaking financial news to measurable market intelligence.**
>
> Market News AI is a full-stack financial intelligence platform that continuously collects market-moving news, understands what happened, identifies the companies and sectors involved, analyzes financial sentiment, connects events with market data, and produces explainable model-based impact estimates across **1D, 3D, and 5D horizons**.

> **AI/model estimate — not investment advice.**
>
> All predictions, signals, probabilities, target levels, sentiment scores, and impact estimates are algorithmically generated and are inherently uncertain.

---

## ✨ What Makes It Different

Market News AI is designed around a simple idea:

**Financial news becomes useful only when it is connected to market context.**
